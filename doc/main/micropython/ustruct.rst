.. pybricks-requirements:: stm32-extra

:mod:`ustruct` -- バイナリデータのパックとアンパック
============================================================

.. automodule:: ustruct

以下のバイトオーダーがサポートされています：

+-----------+------------------------+----------+--------------+
| 文字      | バイトオーダー         | サイズ   | アライメント |
+===========+========================+==========+==============+
| @         | native                 | native   | native       |
+-----------+------------------------+----------+--------------+
| <         | little-endian          | standard | none         |
+-----------+------------------------+----------+--------------+
| >         | big-endian             | standard | none         |
+-----------+------------------------+----------+--------------+
| !         | network (= big-endian) | standard | none         |
+-----------+------------------------+----------+--------------+

以下のデータ型がサポートされています：

+--------+--------------------+-------------+---------------+
| 書式   | C言語の型          | Python型    | 標準サイズ    |
+========+====================+=============+===============+
| b      | signed char        | integer     | 1             |
+--------+--------------------+-------------+---------------+
| B      | unsigned char      | integer     | 1             |
+--------+--------------------+-------------+---------------+
| h      | short              | integer     | 2             |
+--------+--------------------+-------------+---------------+
| H      | unsigned short     | integer     | 2             |
+--------+--------------------+-------------+---------------+
| i      | int                | integer     | 4             |
+--------+--------------------+-------------+---------------+
| I      | unsigned int       | integer     | 4             |
+--------+--------------------+-------------+---------------+
| l      | long               | integer (1) | 4             |
+--------+--------------------+-------------+---------------+
| L      | unsigned long      | integer (1) | 4             |
+--------+--------------------+-------------+---------------+
| q      | long long          | integer (1) | 8             |
+--------+--------------------+-------------+---------------+
| Q      | unsigned long long | integer (1) | 8             |
+--------+--------------------+-------------+---------------+
| f      | float              | float       | 4             |
+--------+--------------------+-------------+---------------+
| d      | double             | float       | 8             |
+--------+--------------------+-------------+---------------+
| s      | char[]             | bytes       |               |
+--------+--------------------+-------------+---------------+
| P      | void *             | integer     |               |
+--------+--------------------+-------------+---------------+

   - \(1\) +/-1073741823 までの値をサポートします。
